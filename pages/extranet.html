Internet Term 9

Extranet

This page explains the extranet using its definition, role, and analogy.

Definition

An extranet is a network that allows limited access to external users outside an organization.

Role

It enables secure collaboration with partners, suppliers, or clients.

Analogy

Like inviting selected outsiders into your private workspace.
