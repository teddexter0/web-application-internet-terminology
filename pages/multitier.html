Internet Term 10

Multitier Architecture

This page explains multitier architecture using its definition, role, and analogy.

Definition

Multitier architecture divides an application into layers such as presentation, application logic, and data storage.

Role

It improves scalability, maintainability, and separation of concerns.

Analogy

Like a restaurant with waiter, chef, and store roles.
