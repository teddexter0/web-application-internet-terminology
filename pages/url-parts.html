Internet Term 18

Parts of a URL

This page explains the parts of a URL using its definition, role, and analogy.

Definition

A URL is made up of parts such as protocol, domain, path, and query.

Role

The protocol shows how data is transferred, the domain is the website name, the path shows the location of the resource, and the query provides additional data.

Analogy

Like a full address that guides someone to a country, town, street, and exact house.
